Market Microstructure

Dynamic AMM Market Elasticity & The "Glut Trap"

Kaggriculture features a dynamic Automated Market Maker where price responds logarithmically or quadratically to transaction volume. Naive bulk liquidation triggers immediate crash to the $1 floor.

Figure 2: Left: Price degradation curves showing how unmetered bulk dumps trigger collapse to the $1 floor. Right: Cumulative gold trajectory demonstrating our sub-cliff drip pacing outperforming bulk dumps by +18.4k coins.

❌ The Unmetered Glut Trap

When greedy agents harvest 30 strawberries and sell them simultaneously on turn 120, the AMM price collapses from $120 to $1 in a single turn. The average realized price drops below $14/unit, destroying over 85% of commodity equity and starving the agent of coin liquidity for subsequent livestock upgrades.

✓ Our Sub-Cliff Paced Drip Solution

Our engine stores harvested produce in on-farm sheds and solves a multi-period dynamic programming knapsack. By selling strictly within the commodity's sub-cliff threshold (e.g. 3-5 units per turn), price elasticity is preserved, recovering an additional +18,400 gold across the 720-turn game.

Micro-Tactics

Opening Lockstep Race Preemption & Slot Hygiene

How EXP-476 neutralized the aggressive LTH-Killer archetype through symmetric round-trip trading and pre-truncation slot consolidation.

Figure 5: Left: EXP-476 lifting win rate against the aggressive LTH-Killer archetype from 20.0% to 72.0%. Right: Pre-truncation order merging eliminating critical transaction drops caused by Kaggle's 10-order ceiling.

1. Symmetric Step-0/1 Round-Trip Preemption

The LTH-Killer opponent executed an early squeeze on wheat on turn 1, starving the opponent of coin equity to buy seeds. Our EXP-476 counter-measure initiates a symmetric 30-unit round trip on step 0, capturing the bid-ask spread and securing guaranteed seed inventory before the opponent's order triggers.

2. Pre-Truncation Order Merging Engine

Kaggle's environment enforces a hard ceiling of 10 orders per turn, silently discarding subsequent trades. EXP-449 attempted merging after truncation (too late). EXP-476 introduced pre-truncation same-item consolidation, guaranteeing zero dropped transactions across 720 turns.

Autonomous Maker-Grader Harness Architecture

A fully automated, self-healing iteration engine utilizing Git worktrees, AST invariant checks, and a permanent failure-vault ledger.

Figure 4: The self-correcting development loop: Hypothesis planning, isolated Git worktree implementation, 20-seed Monte Carlo grading, monotonic CHAMPION ledger promotion, and failure-vault logging.

1. Hypothesis & Worktrees

Every algorithmic change runs in an isolated ephemeral worktree. Main repository stays clean and production-ready at all times.

2. AST Invariant Audit

Before simulation, an AST parser verifies zero syntax faults, zero illegal order combinations, and bounded turn execution budgets.

3. CHAMPION.json Ledger
